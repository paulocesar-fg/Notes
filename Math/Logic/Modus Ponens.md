## Definition

Modus Ponens is a rule of inference that says: 

If
1. $P \to Q$ is true
2. $P$ is true

Then you conclude: 
$$Q \text{ is true}$$


$$P, P \to Q \therefore Q$$


It means "affirming the **antecedent**(hipothesys)".


##### Example: 

1. If it is raining, then the ground is wet.
$$P \to Q$$
2. Assume that It is raining is true.
	$$P \text{ is true}$$  Conclusion
	The ground is wet
	$$Q$$
