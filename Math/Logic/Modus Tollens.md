## Definition

Modus tollens is rule of inference:

If 

1. $P \to Q$ is true
2. $Q$ is false, then ($\neg  Q$) is true

Then you can conclude:
	$$P \text{ is false } (\neg P)$$

$$\neg Q, P \to Q \therefore \neg P$$

It means "denying the consequent"

##### Example

1. If the car has fuel, then it will start;

$$P \to Q$$
2. The car does **not start**
 $$\neg Q$$
 Conclusion: "The car does not have fuel"


$$\neg P$$
This is also _valid_ reasoning.
