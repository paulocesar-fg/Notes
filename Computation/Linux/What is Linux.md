#computation #linux

Linux is an open-source operating system based on the Unix architecture. At its core, it's a **kernel**, which is the part of the system that interacts directly with the hardware, manages resources, and provides a foundation for running software.

Linux was created by **Linus Torvalds**, a Finnish software engineer, in **1991**.

He started it as a personal project while he was a student at the University of Helsinki. His goal was to create a free and open-source alternative to **MINIX**, a Unix-like teaching operating system. Linus released the Linux kernel under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**, which allowed anyone to use, modify, and distribute it.

Since then, thousands of developers around the world have contributed to Linux, making it one of the most widely used operating systems today.
